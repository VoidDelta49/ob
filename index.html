Otep Billiards

Real-physics 8-ball

How to play
  • Aim: drag on the table (←/→ keys, Shift for fine). Use the bar at the bottom for fine aim.
  • Camera: drag up/down to tilt, scroll / pinch to zoom, ⬒ or T for top view (tap the table to aim there).
  • Shoot: pull the power bar down and release, or hold Space.
  • Spin: tap the cue ball (bottom-left) to hit high/low/left/right.
  • Ball in hand: drag the cue ball to place it.
