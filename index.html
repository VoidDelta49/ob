Otep Billiards

Real-physics 8-ball · no aim lines · no power bar

How to play
  • Rack: the player who isn't breaking racks. Drag the rack onto the spot, Tighten, then swipe up to lift it straight off. A crooked lift loosens the rack.
  • Chalk: you must chalk before your first shot. Brush the chalk over the tip. Chalk wears off where the ball touches it, and a dry spot miscues.
  • Aim: drag on the table to walk around the shot. Use the strip at the bottom for fine aim.
  • Tip and butt: drag on the ball face to place the tip. Drag the slider up to raise the butt for a jump or massé. Rails and balls behind the cue force a minimum height.
  • Bridge: Closed, Open, Rail or Mechanical. You get what the position allows: near a rail you use a rail bridge, out of reach you use the mechanical bridge. Shakier bridges cost accuracy.
  • Shoot: tap Get down (Space). Pull your finger down to draw the cue back, then push up through the ball. Your finger speed is the cue speed. Sideways drift steers the cue. There's no power bar.
  • Nerves: the cue sways more on big balls like the 8. Stay down a moment and it settles, but stay too long and your arm tires.
  • Camera: drag up and down to tilt, scroll or pinch to zoom. ⬒ / T gives a top view to look only (you can aim from it in practice).
